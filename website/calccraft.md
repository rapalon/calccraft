Calculators built on floating-point numbers answer `0.1 + 0.2 − 0.3` with `5.55e-17`.
CalcCraft answers `0`.

- **Exact answers.** Numbers are kept as exact fractions, so `1 ÷ 3 × 3` is exactly `1`, and
  Scientific mode shows 32 correct digits.
- **Nothing new to learn.** The layout and keyboard shortcuts of the Windows Calculator:
  Standard, Scientific, Graphing, Programmer and Date modes, plus 12 unit converters.
- **On everything you use.** Apps for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android (in preview), or any
  current browser with nothing to install.
- **Free, no strings.** Open source under the MIT licence, with no ads and no account, and the
  app collects no data.

**Made for** Windows users who want the same calculator on a Mac, on Linux or on a phone,
developers who work in hex and binary, and students graphing functions.

**[Try it in your browser](https://rapalon.github.io/calccraft/)** with nothing to install, or
[download it for your system](#download).
