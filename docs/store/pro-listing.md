## Full description (2,169 characters)

```
CalcCraft Pro is the supporter edition of CalcCraft: the same precise, ad-free calculator, with color themes of its own and no support reminders. Buying it keeps CalcCraft in development.

WHAT PRO ADDS
• Color themes. Gold, Pro's default: the familiar look with a polished gold equals key. Night: dim red on near-black, for using the calculator in the dark without losing your night vision, with no white and no blue anywhere, graphs included. Retro LCD: a greenish LCD display with dark digits. Paper: warm cream and ink. AMOLED Black: pure black everywhere, so an OLED screen keeps those pixels off. High Contrast: white on black with yellow keys and thick outlines, for low vision. More themes will follow.
• No support reminders, ever: no red dot, no popup.
• You support an independent developer and future updates.

EVERYTHING IN CALCCRAFT
• Standard, Scientific, Programmer and Graphing calculators, date calculation, and unit converters for length, area, volume, weight, temperature, energy, speed, time, power, data, pressure and angle.
• Exact arithmetic: 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.3, and 1 ÷ 3 × 3 is 1. Results are exact fractions, not rounded floating point.
• Works like the Windows calculator you know: the same keys, the same rules, the same history and memory.
• Edit any number of a calculation you have typed: tap it in the expression line, change it, and see the new result at once.
• Scientific mode with 32 digits, degrees, radians and gradians, and F-E notation.
• Programmer mode with HEX, DEC, OCT and BIN, byte to QWORD, bitwise operators, shifts and the bit toggling keypad.
• Graphing with equations, inequalities, sliders for variables, tracing, and an analysis of each function (domain, range, zeros, extrema, asymptotes).
• Phone and tablet layouts, portrait and landscape, light and dark, 50 languages, numbers and dates in your phone's regional format, screen reader support, and adjustable key vibration.
• No ads, no account, no tracking, no network access. Your calculations stay on your device.

CalcCraft is open source. The free edition stays free and has every calculator; Pro is for those who want the themes and want to support the work.
```
